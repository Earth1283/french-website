# The Grammar Behind Polite Requests

Hotel French is full of small grammatical moves that make you sound considerate instead of demanding. Most of them come from three things: the *vous* form, the infinitive, and the difference between *des* and *les*.

## *Vous* is the default

In a hotel you address staff with *vous*, the formal “you.” *Pouvez-vous*, *Avez-vous*, *Est-ce que vous* all use the *vous* form. Even if the receptionist is young and friendly, start formal. You can relax later if they make the first move.

## *Pouvez* versus *Je peux*

*Je peux* means “I can” and asks whether something is possible: *Je peux avoir une autre chambre ?* is correct but sounds like you are testing the rules. *Pouvez-vous* asks the staff to do something for you. The difference is one pronoun, and that pronoun changes who has to act.

## *Des* and *les*

*Des serviettes* means “some towels,” an indefinite plural. *Les draps* means “the sheets,” a specific plural. You say *Pouvez-vous apporter des serviettes ?* when you want more towels in general, and *Pouvez-vous changer les draps ?* when you mean the ones on the bed. English makes no such distinction, so this is a real trap. The form *de* appears after negation: *Il n'y a pas de serviettes* (“there are no towels”).

## The conditional *je voudrais*

*Je voudrais* is the conditional of *vouloir*. It softens the request in the same way English “I would like” does. It appears throughout the hotel vocabulary: *Je voudrais partir demain*, *Je voudrais changer de chambre*. Use it whenever you are asking for something rather than stating a preference.

## Gender in the room

*La chambre*, *la vue*, *la climatisation*, *la mer* are feminine. *Le chauffage*, *le parking*, *le minibar* are masculine. *L'ascenseur* is masculine and begins with a vowel, so the article elides: *l'ascenseur* rather than *le ascenseur*. If you learn these with their articles, the agreement takes care of itself.

## Two small checks before you speak

Make sure the infinitive follows *Pouvez-vous* directly. Make sure the *de* in *changer de chambre* is there. Those two details separate a request that gets fulfilled from a request that gets an odd look.
