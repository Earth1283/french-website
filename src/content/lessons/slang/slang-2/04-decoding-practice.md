# Decoding Practice

The best way to learn verlan is to read it in context. Here are short exchanges with the words from this lesson. Read each, guess the meaning, then check the answer.

## Scene 1

> **—** *Tu as vu la nouvelle prof ? Elle est trop sympa.*
>
> **—** *Ouais, mais elle donne des devoirs ouf !*

*Ouf* here means "a crazy amount." Verlan words often act as intensifiers.

## Scene 2

> **—** *Tu viens à la teuf samedi ?*
>
> **—** *Je sais pas. Il y aura ma cousine, et elle est un peu relou.*

*Teuf* means "party." *Relou* means "annoying."

## Scene 3

> **—** *Il y a un keum qui me suit depuis tout à l'heure.*
>
> **—** *Il est chelou ?*
>
> **—** *Oui, un peu.*

*Keum* is "guy," and *chelou* is "shady, weird."

## Scene 4

> **—** *C'est ma meuf, Camille.*
>
> **—** *Enchanté !*

*Meuf* here means "girlfriend." It can also just mean "woman."

## Vocabulary check

Match each verlan word to its meaning:

| Verlan | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1. *ouf* | a. annoying |
| 2. *relou* | b. crazy |
| 3. *chelou* | c. woman |
| 4. *meuf* | d. shady |
| 5. *keum* | e. guy |

**Answers:** 1-b, 2-a, 3-d, 4-c, 5-e.

## Translate

1. This party is crazy.
2. The guy is weird.
3. It's annoying.

**Answers:** 1. *Cette teuf est ouf.* 2. *Le keum est chelou.* 3. *C'est relou.*

## Keep listening

Verlan changes over time. Words come and go. Listen to French rap, watch recent French films, and pay attention to how friends speak. When a word seems new, ask: *Qu'est-ce que ça veut dire, "zarbi" ?* You will usually get a smile and an explanation. That kind of curiosity is the best way to keep your French alive.
