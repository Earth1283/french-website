# State Dietary Needs Without Ambiguity

Preferences and allergies require different language. A preference can be negotiated; an allergy needs to be understood as a safety issue. State it early, clearly, and confirm the specific ingredient.

## Vegetarian and vegan language

> *Je suis végétarien / végétarienne.* — I am vegetarian.
>
> *Je suis végétalien / végétalienne.* — I am vegan.
>
> *Je ne mange pas de viande ni de poisson.* — I eat neither meat nor fish.

The everyday adjective *vegan* is also widely recognized, but *végétalien(ne)* is precise French. Do not rely only on the word *végétarien* if fish or meat stock matters to you. Ask about the actual ingredients.

## Allergy language

Use *Je suis allergique à…* followed by the ingredient:

- *aux arachides* — peanuts
- *aux fruits à coque* — tree nuts
- *au lait* — milk
- *aux œufs* — eggs
- *au gluten* — gluten
- *aux fruits de mer* — shellfish/seafood

The article changes because *à* combines with *le* and *les*: *à + le → au* and *à + les → aux*. Before a feminine singular noun, use *à la*: *allergique à la moutarde*.

For a severe allergy, say:

> *J'ai une allergie grave aux arachides. Même une petite quantité est dangereuse.*
>
> I have a severe peanut allergy. Even a small amount is dangerous.

Then ask a direct question: *Est-ce qu'il y a des arachides dans ce plat ?* (“Are there peanuts in this dish?”). *Sans arachides* means “without peanuts,” but asking what the dish contains gives you more information than simply requesting an omission.

## Useful ingredient questions

> *Qu'est-ce qu'il y a dans ce plat ?* — What is in this dish?
>
> *Est-ce que c'est préparé avec du beurre ?* — Is it prepared with butter?
>
> *Est-ce que la sauce contient de la viande ?* — Does the sauce contain meat?
>
> *Pouvez-vous vérifier en cuisine ?* — Can you check with the kitchen?

If a medical risk is involved, use a translated allergy card and verify with staff rather than treating a lesson as a guarantee. The language here helps you communicate the risk precisely.
