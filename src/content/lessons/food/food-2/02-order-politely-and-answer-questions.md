# Order Politely and Handle the Follow-Up

The safest ordering frame is *Je voudrais…* (“I would like…”). It is polite, complete, and works for one item or a whole meal.

> *Je voudrais le poulet, s'il vous plaît.* — I would like the chicken, please.
>
> *Je voudrais le menu à vingt-cinq euros.* — I would like the twenty-five-euro set menu.
>
> *Pour moi, ce sera le poisson.* — For me, it will be the fish.

*Je veux* literally means “I want.” It is grammatically correct but can sound abrupt when directed at staff. Use *je voudrais* until casual context gives you a reason to shorten it.

## Questions a server may ask

Ordering triggers predictable follow-ups. Learn them as complete chunks:

| You hear | They are asking |
|---|---|
| *Vous avez choisi ?* | Have you chosen? |
| *Qu'est-ce que vous prenez ?* | What will you have? |
| *Et comme boisson ?* | And to drink? |
| *Quelle cuisson ?* | How would you like it cooked? |
| *Sur place ou à emporter ?* | For here or to go? |
| *Vous désirez autre chose ?* | Would you like anything else? |

If you need time, say *Pas encore, merci* (“Not yet, thank you”) or *Encore une minute, s'il vous plaît* (“One more minute, please”). If you missed the question, use *Pardon, pouvez-vous répéter ?*

## Steak doneness

For beef, common choices move from less cooked to more cooked:

- *bleu* — very rare
- *saignant* — rare
- *à point* — medium to medium-well, depending on the restaurant
- *bien cuit* — well done

Cooking expectations vary, so point or clarify if it matters. *Pas trop cuit* means “not too well cooked.”

## Ask for a recommendation

*Qu'est-ce que vous recommandez ?* invites guidance. Make it specific for a more useful answer: *Qu'est-ce que vous recommandez comme plat végétarien ?* (“What do you recommend as a vegetarian main?”) or *Quel est le plat du jour ?* (“What is today’s special?”).

You do not need to understand every word in the reply. Listen for the dish name, the main ingredient, and one description, then confirm: *D'accord, je vais prendre ça* (“All right, I’ll have that”).
