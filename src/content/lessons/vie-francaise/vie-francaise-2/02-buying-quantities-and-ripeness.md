# Buying: Quantities and Ripeness

At the market, you rarely say *I want a tomato*. You buy by weight, by bunch, or by piece, and you say when you will eat it. The vocabulary is small and repetitive, which makes it easy to learn.

## Units

| French | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *un kilo de…* | a kilo of… |
| *une livre de…* | half a kilo (500 g) |
| *deux cents grammes de…* | 200 grams of… |
| *une botte de…* | a bunch of… (carrots, radishes) |
| *une barquette de…* | a punnet of… (strawberries) |
| *une pièce* | one piece |
| *une tranche de…* | a slice of… |
| *un morceau de…* | a piece of… |

All of these take *de*, with no article:

> *Un kilo de tomates, s'il vous plaît.*
>
> *Une livre de cerises.*
>
> *Un morceau de comté.*

## Asking and answering

> **—** *Bonjour, madame. Je voudrais un kilo de pommes.*
>
> **—** *Lesquelles ? Les golden ou les gala ?*
>
> **—** *Les gala, s'il vous plaît.*
>
> **—** *Et avec ça ?*
>
> **—** *Ce sera tout, merci.*

*Et avec ça ?* ("Anything else with that?") and *Ce sera tout* ("That'll be all") are the two phrases that structure every purchase.

## Adjusting the amount

- *un peu plus* — a little more
- *un peu moins* — a little less
- *C'est parfait.* — That's perfect.

If the seller puts a little too much on the scale, say *un peu moins, s'il vous plaît.*

## Ripeness

Because the seller selects, tell them when you plan to eat it:

| French | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *pour ce soir* | for tonight |
| *pour demain* | for tomorrow |
| *pour ce week-end* | for the weekend |
| *bien mûr* | very ripe |
| *pas trop mûr* | not too ripe |
| *à point* | perfectly ripe, ready to eat |

> *Un avocat pour ce soir, bien mûr.* — An avocado for tonight, very ripe.

For cheese, *à point* is a term of art: a cheese that is ready to eat at its best.

## What the seller may ask

- *C'est pour manger quand ?* — When will you eat it?
- *Je vous le mets où ?* — Where shall I put it? (in which bag)
- *Vous voulez que je vous le coupe ?* — Shall I cut it for you?

Have a bag ready, or say *Dans ce sac, s'il vous plaît.*
