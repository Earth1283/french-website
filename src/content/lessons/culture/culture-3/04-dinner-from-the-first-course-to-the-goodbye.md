# Dinner from the First Course to the Goodbye

Here is a realistic dinner with friends, from the moment the host calls everyone to the table to the moment you leave. Watch how each phrase keeps the meal in order.

## Sitting down

> **Host:** *Passez à table !*
>
> **You:** *Merci. Ça sent très bon.*
>
> **Host:** *Voilà l'entrée. Bon appétit !*
>
> **You:** *Bon appétit à tous !*

*Passez à table* means “come to the table,” and it is the host's signal to sit. *Ça sent très bon* means “it smells very good,” a compliment that is always welcome. When the host says *Bon appétit*, you may begin, and you can reply with the same phrase to the whole table.

## Ordering the main course and the cheese

> **Host:** *Vous voulez encore du plat principal ?*
>
> **You:** *Un petit peu, merci. C'était délicieux.*
>
> **Host:** *Et le fromage ?*
>
> **You:** *Oui, je prends un peu de fromage, s'il vous plaît.*

*Encore* means “more” or “again,” and *un petit peu* means “a little.” Saying *C'était délicieux* after the main course is an appropriate compliment, and the past tense shows you have finished the dish.

## When you have had enough

> **Host:** *Vous voulez encore du dessert ?*
>
> **You:** *Non merci, je n'en veux plus. Je suis plein(e).*

*Je n'en veux plus* means “I do not want any more.” *Je suis plein(e)* means “I am full,” and it is a polite way to decline without giving a long explanation. Add the feminine *-e* if you are a woman.

## Leaving

> **You:** *Merci pour cette soirée. C'était vraiment convivial.*
>
> **Host:** *Avec plaisir. Revenez quand vous voulez !*
>
> **You:** *Merci, au revoir et bonne soirée !*

*Convivial* is the perfect word for the evening. *Revenez quand vous voulez* is an invitation to return whenever you like, and it is a warm way to finish.

## If something goes wrong

If you started eating before *Bon appétit*, say *Oups, j'ai commencé trop tôt* and laugh about it. If you were asked a question you did not follow, say *Pardon, je n'ai pas bien compris. Vous pouvez répéter ?* The sequence is simple: sit, eat, speak clearly, and thank the host at the end.
