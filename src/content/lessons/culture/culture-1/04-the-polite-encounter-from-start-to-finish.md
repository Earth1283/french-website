# The Polite Encounter from Start to Finish

Here is a realistic sequence: meeting a friend's family, buying bread the next morning, and recovering from a mistake. Read each line for the social work it does, not just its translation.

## Meeting the family

> **You:** *Bonsoir. Enchanté de faire votre connaissance.*
>
> **Friend's mother:** *Enchantée aussi. Entrez, je vous en prie.*
>
> **You:** *Merci beaucoup. Excusez-moi, où puis-je poser mon manteau ?*
>
> **Friend's mother:** *Par là, à gauche.*

*Enchanté de faire votre connaissance* is the longer, more formal form of “pleased to meet you.” The mother replies in the feminine, *enchantée*, and offers you her welcome with *je vous en prie*. Your question about the coat starts with *excusez-moi*, which is the right opening for a request to someone you have just met.

## Buying bread the next morning

> **You:** *Bonjour, Madame. Une baguette, s'il vous plaît.*
>
> **Baker:** *Voilà. Ce sera tout ?*
>
> **You:** *Oui, merci. Bonne journée.*
>
> **Baker:** *Bonne journée, Monsieur.*

The greeting comes before the request, and the request ends with *s'il vous plaît*. The baker asks *Ce sera tout ?* (“Will that be all?”), which signals that the transaction is nearly complete. *Bonne journée* is a warm closing that matches the morning greeting.

## Recovering from a mistake

Suppose you used *tu* with the older baker by mistake. Do not panic. Say:

> *Pardon, je vous ai tutoyé. Je suis désolé(e).*
>
> Sorry, I used the informal *tu* with you. I apologise.

*Tutoyer* is the verb meaning to use *tu* with someone, and *je vous ai tutoyé* is the past tense. Naming the mistake and apologising is more effective than pretending it did not happen. Most people will accept the apology and move on.

## The reusable pattern

Greet, use *vous* unless invited to use *tu*, make the request with *s'il vous plaît*, thank the person, and close politely. If you make a mistake, name it, apologise, and continue. The structure is short, and it covers nearly every encounter you will have.
