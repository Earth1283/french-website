# Reading Signs and Opening Hours

A French shop door is covered with information: when it opens, when it closes, and when it will not open at all. Learning the vocabulary lets you decode it in seconds.

## Key words

| French | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *ouvert* | open |
| *fermé* | closed |
| *horaires d'ouverture* | opening hours |
| *fermeture hebdomadaire* | weekly closing day |
| *fermeture annuelle* | annual closure (holiday) |
| *ouvert 7 jours sur 7* | open every day |
| *en continu* | no lunch break |
| *sauf* | except |

## Reading a timetable

> *Du lundi au vendredi : 9h–12h et 14h–18h30*
>
> *Samedi : 9h–12h*
>
> *Fermé dimanche et jours fériés*

Translate it: Monday to Friday from nine to twelve and two to six thirty; Saturday morning only; closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Pay attention to the **two blocks** each weekday. The gap between them is the lunch break. A common mistake is to see "9h–18h30" and assume continuous service.

## Phrases you will see

- *Fermé pour congés.* — Closed for holidays.
- *Fermé pour travaux.* — Closed for renovation.
- *Ouvert exceptionnellement.* — Exceptionally open.
- *Retour dans cinq minutes.* — Back in five minutes.
- *Pharmacie de garde.* — Duty pharmacy (open outside normal hours).

*La pharmacie de garde* is useful. When a pharmacy is closed, its door often shows the nearest one that is open.

## Asking

If the sign is unclear, ask:

> *Vous êtes ouverts le dimanche ?* — Are you open on Sundays?
>
> *Vous fermez à quelle heure ?* — What time do you close?
>
> *Vous ouvrez à quelle heure demain ?* — What time do you open tomorrow?

The *vous* form refers to the business as a group of people, which is natural in French.

## Online hours

Websites and map apps show opening hours, but they can be out of date, especially for small shops. A phone call is often the most reliable check: *Bonjour, vous êtes ouverts cet après-midi ?*

## Practice

What does this sign say?

> *Boulangerie. Fermeture hebdomadaire le lundi. Ouvert de 7h à 13h30 et de 16h à 19h30.*

**Answer:** Bakery. Closed every Monday. Open from 7:00 to 13:30 and from 16:00 to 19:30.
