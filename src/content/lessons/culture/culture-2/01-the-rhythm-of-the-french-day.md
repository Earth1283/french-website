# The Rhythm of the French Day and Week

If you arrive in France expecting everything to be open all day, every day, you will be surprised. Daily life runs on a rhythm: a long lunch, closed Sundays in many places, and a calendar of public holidays. Learning the rhythm is as useful as learning the words.

## The lunch break

*La pause déjeuner* is a real institution. In smaller towns and in many shops, **doors close around noon and reopen at 14h or 15h**. You may find a pharmacy, a bakery, or a post office closed at 13h on a weekday.

Typical hours for a small shop:

> *Ouvert du mardi au samedi, de 9h à 12h30 et de 14h30 à 19h.*

Larger chains and shopping centres, especially in cities, are often open "non-stop" (*en continu*).

## Sunday and Monday

- **Sunday:** many shops are closed, especially outside big cities. Bakeries are open in the morning, and local markets often run.
- **Monday:** a common closing day for smaller shops, restaurants, and some museums. Always check before you go.
- **Museums:** most national museums close one day a week. The Louvre is closed on Tuesday, and the Musée d'Orsay on Monday.

Confirm opening days on a website before planning a visit.

## Public holidays: *les jours fériés*

France has eleven public holidays. The main ones:

| Date | Holiday |
|---|---|
| 1 January | *le jour de l'An* |
| Spring | *le lundi de Pâques* |
| 1 May | *la fête du Travail* |
| 8 May | *la Victoire 1945* |
| Spring | *l'Ascension* |
| Spring | *le lundi de Pentecôte* |
| 14 July | *la fête nationale* |
| 15 August | *l'Assomption* |
| 1 November | *la Toussaint* |
| 11 November | *l'Armistice* |
| 25 December | *Noël* |

Public transport runs on reduced schedules, and many shops close.

## *Les ponts* and *les vacances*

When a holiday falls on a Thursday, many people take Friday off too: this is *faire le pont* (making the bridge). Roads and trains are busy on bridge weekends.

August is the main holiday month, and many small businesses close for two or three weeks (*fermeture annuelle*). Cities feel quieter, and some restaurants may be closed.

## What to do

Plan around the rhythm instead of fighting it:

- do errands in the morning
- eat lunch like everyone else
- buy bread and food before Sunday
- check holiday dates before you travel
