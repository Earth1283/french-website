# Imparfait Paints; Passé Composé Acts

## Retell it from another angle

Take one simple event and change the camera position. *Le téléphone a sonné* is a completed event. Put it inside a scene: *Je lisais tranquillement quand le téléphone a sonné.* Then change the background: *Pendant que le téléphone sonnait, je cherchais mes clés.* The event has become an ongoing sound, so imparfait works. This exercise shows that tense does not belong permanently to a verb. It belongs to the role that action plays in the story you are telling at that moment.

French past narration has two camera settings. The imparfait shows a situation in progress, a habit, a description, or the background. The passé composé records a bounded event that moves the story forward. Neither is “the English past tense”; choose according to the job the verb has in the story.

> *Il pleuvait et la rue était vide.* — It was raining and the street was empty.

> *Tout à coup, le bus est arrivé.* — Suddenly, the bus arrived.

The rain and empty street form the scene. The bus arrives as one completed event. That contrast is the heart of the choice.

## Use imparfait for

- repeated habits: *Quand j'étais enfant, je lisais beaucoup.*
- ongoing actions: *Je travaillais quand tu as appelé.*
- descriptions: *Le café était petit et bruyant.*
- time, age, weather, feelings: *Il était huit heures; j'avais faim.*

## Use passé composé for

- one finished action: *J'ai perdu mes clés.*
- a beginning or ending: *La réunion a commencé à neuf heures.*
- an interruption: *Le téléphone a sonné.*
- a sequence of plot events: *Elle est entrée, a regardé autour d'elle et est partie.*

> *Je regardais un film quand Marie a téléphoné.* — I was watching a film when Marie called.

Do not treat the imparfait as a weaker past. It gives a story its atmosphere and duration. The passé composé is the moment that changes something. Together they let you say more than a chain of “then I did this” sentences.

## A useful question

Ask: “Was this the setting or a completed change?” If it was already happening, habitual, descriptive, or unfinished, reach for imparfait. If it happened as a whole and advances the timeline, reach for passé composé. Context beats memorised English translations every time.
