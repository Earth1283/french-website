# Opinion Verbs Change Mood with Meaning

## Say what you mean, not what a chart guesses

Try each pair with an actual opinion: *Je pense que ce restaurant est bon* and *Je ne pense pas que ce restaurant soit bon*. The first commits to an evaluation; the second distances you from it. Then make a question: *Penses-tu qu'il viendra ?* Notice whether you are asking for a prediction or openly wondering about the possibility. Native usage allows nuance and variation, but your sentence should always have a communicative reason. Mood is a signal of stance. If you can explain that stance in English, you can usually make a confident French choice.

Affirmative *penser que* and *croire que* normally take the indicative because the speaker presents the statement as accepted information. Negate or question the opinion, and doubt enters the room; the subjunctive becomes common, especially in careful French.

> *Je pense qu'il a raison.* — I think he is right.

> *Je ne pense pas qu'il ait raison.* — I do not think he is right.

The first sentence asserts belief. The second withholds it. French grammar follows that difference in stance, not an arbitrary list of verbs.

## Questions are nuanced

> *Penses-tu qu'il viendra ?* — Do you think he will come?

> *Penses-tu qu'il vienne ?* — Do you think he is likely to come?

Both forms can occur. Indicative often treats the answer as a straightforward prediction; subjunctive highlights uncertainty or distance. In everyday speech, usage varies, so do not treat a single question form as a moral test. In formal writing, match the nuance you mean.

## Verbs of certainty

*Savoir que, constater que, être certain que* normally take the indicative.

> *Je sais qu'elle est arrivée.* — I know she arrived.

But negative *ne pas être certain que* naturally brings subjunctive: *Je ne suis pas certain qu'elle soit arrivée.* The same movement appears again: certainty gives indicative; doubt opens the door to subjunctive.

## A trap to avoid

Do not use subjunctive after every opinion just to sound advanced. *Je crois qu'il est là* is standard. The elegant choice is the meaningful one. Ask whether you are affirming a proposition or marking it as uncertain, contested, wished for, or emotionally evaluated.
