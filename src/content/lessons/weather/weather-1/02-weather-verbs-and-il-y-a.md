# Weather Verbs and *Il y a*

Not all weather uses *faire*. Rain, snow, and storms have their own verbs, and many other phenomena use the structure *il y a* ("there is"). This chapter completes the toolkit.

## Verbs that only exist in the *il* form

| French | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Il pleut.* | It's raining. |
| *Il neige.* | It's snowing. |
| *Il gèle.* | It's freezing. |
| *Il grêle.* | It's hailing. |
| *Il tonne.* | It's thundering. |

These are **impersonal verbs**. They exist only in the third person singular. You will never conjugate *pleuvoir* for *je* or *nous*, which is why *il pleut* is one of the first things you learn.

## Things that happen: *il y a*

When a weather phenomenon is a noun, French uses *il y a*:

> *Il y a du vent.* — It's windy.
>
> *Il y a du soleil.* — It's sunny.
>
> *Il y a du brouillard.* — It's foggy.
>
> *Il y a des nuages.* — It's cloudy (there are clouds).
>
> *Il y a un orage.* — There's a storm.
>
> *Il y a des éclairs.* — There's lightning.

Both *Il y a du vent* and *Il fait du vent* are heard. *Il y a* is the safer choice for beginners, because it works for all these nouns without exception.

## Describing the sky

The sky itself takes *être*:

- *Le ciel est bleu.* — The sky is blue.
- *Le ciel est couvert.* — The sky is overcast.
- *Le ciel est dégagé.* — The sky is clear.
- *Le temps est nuageux.* — It's cloudy.

## Intensity and idioms

| French | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Il pleut un peu.* | It's drizzling. |
| *Il pleut à verse.* | It's pouring. |
| *Il pleut des cordes.* | It's raining cats and dogs. |
| *Il pleut beaucoup.* | It's raining a lot. |
| *Quel temps de chien !* | What awful weather! |

*Il pleut des cordes* literally says "it's raining ropes."

## *Le temps*: weather and time

*Le temps* means both "the weather" and "time." Context separates them:

- *Quel temps fait-il ?* — What's the weather like?
- *Je n'ai pas le temps.* — I don't have time.

When someone says *Il fait quel temps ?* they want weather. When they say *Tu as le temps ?*, they want a moment of your time.

## Summary table

| You want to say | Use |
|---|---|
| hot, cold, nice, bad | *il fait* + adj |
| rain, snow | *il pleut / il neige* |
| wind, sun, fog, clouds | *il y a* + noun |
| the sky | *le ciel est* + adj |
