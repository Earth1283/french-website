# Asking About the Weather and the Forecast

Weather is the universal small-talk subject, and French has a handful of questions and phrases that keep it flowing. This chapter covers questions, forecasts, and talking about tomorrow.

## Asking

| Question | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Quel temps fait-il ?* | What's the weather like? |
| *Il fait quel temps ?* | same, casual |
| *Quel temps fait-il chez vous ?* | What's the weather like where you are? |
| *Il fait combien de degrés ?* | What's the temperature? |

*Quel temps fait-il aujourd'hui à Lyon ?* works perfectly well for a stranger, a colleague, or a phone call to a friend.

## The forecast: *la météo*

*La météo* is the weather forecast. Radio, TV, and phone apps all use the word.

> *Qu'est-ce que dit la météo ?* — What does the forecast say?
>
> *D'après la météo, il va pleuvoir demain.* — According to the forecast, it's going to rain tomorrow.

*D'après* and *selon* both mean "according to."

## Talking about tomorrow

The easiest way to talk about the future is *aller* + infinitive:

> *Il va pleuvoir.* — It's going to rain.
>
> *Il va neiger ce week-end.* — It's going to snow this weekend.
>
> *Il va faire beau demain.* — It's going to be nice tomorrow.
>
> *Il va faire chaud.* — It's going to be hot.

Notice the infinitive *faire* after *va*: *il va faire beau*. This is the same structure you will meet in the lesson on plans.

## Past weather

A simple way to talk about yesterday:

- *Hier, il a plu toute la journée.* — Yesterday it rained all day.
- *Hier, il faisait très chaud.* — Yesterday it was very hot.

You do not need to master these forms now, but recognizing them helps. *Il a plu* comes from *pleuvoir*, and *il faisait* is the imperfect of *faire*.

## Small-talk gambits

French speakers use weather to start conversations:

- *Il fait beau, hein ?* — Nice weather, isn't it?
- *Quelle chaleur !* — What heat!
- *Quel froid !* — How cold!
- *Il fait un temps magnifique.* — The weather is magnificent.

The reply is often just *Ah oui !* or *C'est vrai !* A short, agreeing answer is exactly right.

## Say it back

Describe today's weather in one sentence, tomorrow's in another:

> *Aujourd'hui, il fait doux et il y a du vent. Demain, il va pleuvoir.*
