# Why *Deux Places* Has No Article

The cinema phrases look simple, but each one follows a pattern you can reuse for other purchases. Once you see the pattern, you can ask for a different film, a different time, or a different number of tickets without memorising a new sentence.

## Numbers before nouns, no article

*Deux places* means two seats or tickets. There is no article (*les*, *des*, *un*) because you are counting, not referring to specific items. The same logic works for *trois places*, *quatre places*, and so on. For one ticket, use the singular noun with *une*: *une place, s'il vous plaît*.

Compare this with a specific reference: *les places sont là-bas* means “the seats are over there.” The article appears when you point at something known; it disappears when you are simply stating a quantity.

## Gender and agreement

*La version* is feminine, so the adjectives must agree: *la version originale* and *la version française*. The masculine form *le film français* is a different phrase, which is why you should not mix them. When you describe the film itself, use the masculine: *le film est en anglais* (“the film is in English”).

*Une séance* is also feminine. In *à quelle heure est la prochaine séance ?*, the adjective *prochaine* takes the feminine ending. You will hear *le prochain film* if you ask about the next film, and *la prochaine séance* if you ask about the next showing.

## Pronunciation: *s'il vous plaît*

Do not pronounce every letter. *S'il* is said as one syllable, *sil*, and *vous plaît* sounds close to *vou plè*. The final *s* of *vous* is silent here. Speakers often shorten the whole phrase to a fast *s'il vous plaît*, which is perfectly normal.

## Abbreviations on the listing

*VO* is pronounced as two letters: *vé-o*. *VF* is *vé-èf*. Learn to read them as letters rather than as words. The same applies to *VOST*, which some people say as one word, *vo-st*.

The useful insight is that French booking language is mostly about quantities and categories. Learn the frame, then change the noun.
