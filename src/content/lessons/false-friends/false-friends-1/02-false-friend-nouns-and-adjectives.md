# False Friends: Nouns and Adjectives

The most common false friends are nouns and adjectives. They appear in menus, signs, and conversation, so learning them early saves you from mistakes and confusion.

## Nouns

| French | Looks like | Actually means | The English word is… |
|---|---|---|---|
| *librairie* | library | bookshop | *bibliothèque* |
| *préservatif* | preservative | condom | *conservateur* |
| *location* | location | rental | *emplacement* / *endroit* |
| *lecture* | lecture | reading | *cours* / *conférence* |
| *chance* | chance | luck | *possibilité* |
| *monnaie* | money | change (coins) | *argent* |
| *déception* | deception | disappointment | *tromperie* |
| *journée* | journey | day | *voyage* / *trajet* |

*Location* is worth noticing: *une location de voiture* is a car rental, a sign you will see at every airport.

## Adjectives

| French | Looks like | Actually means | The English word is… |
|---|---|---|---|
| *sensible* | sensible | sensitive | *raisonnable* |
| *gentil(le)* | gentle | kind, nice | *doux / douce* |
| *sympathique* | sympathetic | nice, friendly | *compatissant* |
| *blessé(e)* | blessed | injured | *béni(e)* |
| *actuel(le)* | actual | current | *réel / véritable* |
| *ancien(ne)* | ancient | former, old | *antique* |

*Gentil* is a fine example. A friend who says *Tu es très gentil* is complimenting you for being kind, not gentle. To say "gentle," use *doux*.

*Sympathique*, often shortened to *sympa*, describes someone you like. Using it to mean "I sympathize" would be wrong.

## *Ancien* before and after

*Ancien* changes meaning with position:

- *un ancien professeur* — a former teacher
- *un bâtiment ancien* — an old building

A lesson learned here helps with *propre*, *seul*, and *cher* too, which also shift meaning around the noun.

## Be careful with *sensible*

This one causes real misunderstandings in a business setting:

> *Il est très sensible.* — He is very sensitive.

If you want to say someone is sensible or reasonable, use *raisonnable*, *sage*, or *de bon sens*.

## Quick check

Which French word fits?

1. I am looking for a bookshop. → *Je cherche une ___.*
2. She is kind. → *Elle est ___.*
3. The doctor says I am injured. → *Je suis ___.*

**Answers:** 1. *librairie*. 2. *gentille*. 3. *blessé(e)*.
