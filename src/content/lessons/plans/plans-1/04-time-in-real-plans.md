# Time in Real Plans: Durations, Lateness, and Appointments

Knowing how to say the clock time is half the work. Real plans also need durations, lateness, and a vocabulary for being on time.

## How long

| French | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *une heure* | one hour |
| *une demi-heure* | half an hour |
| *un quart d'heure* | a quarter of an hour |
| *dix minutes* | ten minutes |
| *deux heures et demie* | two and a half hours |

> *Le trajet dure une heure et quart.* — The journey takes an hour and a quarter.

## From… to…, in…

- *de neuf heures à midi* — from nine to noon
- *dans une heure* — in one hour (from now)
- *il y a une heure* — an hour ago
- *pendant deux heures* — for two hours

*Dans une heure* means "an hour from now." A learner who says *en une heure* is saying "within an hour" (that is, taking an hour to finish).

## Early, late, on time

- *à l'heure* — on time
- *en avance* — early
- *en retard* — late
- *tôt* / *tard* — early / late in the day

> *Désolé, je suis en retard !*
>
> *Pas de problème, vous êtes à l'heure.*

*Je suis en retard* describes you. *Le train a du retard* describes the train. *Il y a un retard* is the standard announcement.

## Making a plan

> **—** *On se retrouve demain ?*
>
> **—** *Oui, à quelle heure ?*
>
> **—** *Vers dix-neuf heures, devant le cinéma.*
>
> **—** *Parfait. À demain !*

## French punctuality

For a business appointment or a train, be on time. For a dinner at someone's home, **arriving ten to fifteen minutes late** is usual, and arriving exactly on time can catch the host unprepared. For a party, even longer is normal.

## An exercise

Translate:

1. The shop opens at nine thirty in the morning.
2. The film starts at a quarter to eight in the evening.
3. See you in an hour.

**Answers:** 1. *Le magasin ouvre à neuf heures et demie du matin* (or *neuf heures trente*). 2. *Le film commence à huit heures moins le quart du soir* (or *dix-neuf heures quarante-cinq*). 3. *On se voit dans une heure.*
