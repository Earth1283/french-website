# The 24-Hour Clock and Parts of the Day

France uses two systems side by side. Everyday conversation uses a 12-hour feel, with words to say morning or evening. Timetables, appointments, train tickets, and official documents use a full 24-hour clock.

## The formal system

At 13:00 and beyond, you simply keep counting:

| Written | Spoken |
|---|---|
| *13h* | *treize heures* |
| *14h30* | *quatorze heures trente* |
| *18h45* | *dix-huit heures quarante-cinq* |
| *21h15* | *vingt et une heures quinze* |
| *00h05* | *zéro heure cinq* |

Notice that the formal system does **not** use *et quart*, *et demie*, or *moins le quart*. It just says the number of minutes: *quatorze heures trente*, not *quatorze heures et demie*.

The written form uses a lowercase **h** as separator: *14h30*, not *14:30* (though you will see both).

## Converting between systems

To convert 24-hour time to everyday speech, subtract 12 from anything over 12:

- *15h* → three in the afternoon → *trois heures de l'après-midi*
- *20h* → eight in the evening → *huit heures du soir*

## Adding the part of the day

When you use the 12-hour system, add a phrase to say which half:

| Phrase | Meaning | Typical range |
|---|---|---|
| *du matin* | in the morning | until noon |
| *de l'après-midi* | in the afternoon | noon until about 18h |
| *du soir* | in the evening | from about 18h |
| *de la nuit* | at night | late evening and early morning |

> *Le train part à sept heures du matin.* — The train leaves at 7 a.m.
>
> *On dîne à huit heures du soir.* — We have dinner at 8 p.m.

You do not use *a.m.* or *p.m.* in French.

## Where you meet each system

- **Shop hours:** *ouvert de 9h à 19h*
- **Train timetables:** *départ 14h37*
- **Cinema listings:** *séance de 20h45*
- **Casual invitations:** *on se retrouve à huit heures et demie*

When you read a schedule, assume 24-hour. When you chat, assume everyday speech. Learn to hear both.

## Quick test

What time is it?

1. *Il est dix-sept heures trente.*
2. *Il est huit heures moins le quart du matin.*

**Answers:** 1. 5:30 p.m. (17:30). 2. 7:45 a.m.
