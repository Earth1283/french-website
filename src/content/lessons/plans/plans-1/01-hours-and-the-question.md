# Hours and the Basic Question

Telling time in French follows a pattern you can learn in ten minutes: a fixed question, a fixed answer frame, and two special words for noon and midnight. Everything else is numbers you already know.

## Asking the time

There are three common ways to ask:

| Question | Register |
|---|---|
| *Quelle heure est-il ?* | standard |
| *Il est quelle heure ?* | casual |
| *Vous avez l'heure, s'il vous plaît ?* | polite, to a stranger |

The third one is a favorite with strangers. It literally asks "Do you have the time?" and is a gentle way to approach someone in the street.

## The answer frame: *Il est… heure(s)*

The subject is always *il* (it), and the hour is feminine:

> *Il est une heure.* — It's one o'clock.
>
> *Il est deux heures.* — It's two o'clock.
>
> *Il est dix heures.* — It's ten o'clock.

Note the singular *heure* for one o'clock and the plural *heures* for every other hour. Spoken, you hear no difference between *heure* and *heures*, but the written form matters.

## Noon and midnight have their own words

In everyday speech French has two words for these moments, and uses them instead of *douze heures* or *zéro heure*:

- *midi* — noon
- *minuit* — midnight

> *Il est midi.* — It's noon.
>
> *Il est minuit.* — It's midnight.

Neither takes *heures*. It would sound odd to say *midi heures*.

## "At" a time: *à*

To say something happens at a certain time, use *à*:

> *Le film commence à huit heures.* — The movie starts at eight.
>
> *Le train part à midi.* — The train leaves at noon.
>
> *Le magasin ferme à dix-huit heures.* — The shop closes at six.

And the question that goes with it:

> *À quelle heure est le dîner ?* — What time is dinner?
>
> *À quelle heure part le train ?* — What time does the train leave?

Don't confuse *Quelle heure est-il ?* (what time is it now) with *À quelle heure… ?* (at what time does something happen). The *à* marks the event.

## Approximations

- *vers* + hour: *vers huit heures* — around eight
- *pile*: *à huit heures pile* — at eight sharp
- *environ* or *autour de*: *autour de midi*

> *On se retrouve vers huit heures ?* — Shall we meet around eight?
