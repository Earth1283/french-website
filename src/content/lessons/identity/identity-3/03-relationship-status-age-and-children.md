# Status, Age, and Children

Once you know the family words, you can say who you are in terms of relationships. This chapter covers the status words, how to give an age, and how to talk about children, including two grammar points that surprise learners.

## Relationship status

| French | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *célibataire* | single |
| *en couple* | in a relationship |
| *marié(e)* | married |
| *pacsé(e)* | in a civil partnership (PACS) |
| *divorcé(e)* | divorced |
| *veuf / veuve* | widowed |

*Pacsé* comes from the *PACS*, a French civil union open to any couple. Many couples choose it over marriage, so you will hear it often.

> *Je suis marié depuis dix ans.* — I've been married for ten years.

## Partner words

*Mon copain / ma copine* means "my boyfriend / girlfriend," though *copain* can also mean "buddy." *Mon petit ami / ma petite amie* is clearer. For a spouse, *mon mari / ma femme* or the neutral *mon conjoint / ma conjointe*.

A note on **mon ami / mon amie**: these usually mean friend, but can imply a romantic partner. Say *un ami* for a friend, and *mon copain* if you mean a boyfriend.

## Age: you *have* years

French does not use "to be" for age. It uses *avoir*:

> *J'ai trente ans.* — I'm thirty (literally "I have thirty years").
>
> *Il a quarante-deux ans.* — He's forty-two.
>
> *Quel âge avez-vous ?* — How old are you?

Never say *Je suis trente ans*. It is one of the first things teachers correct.

## Children and the negative *de*

Positive sentences use the number:

> *J'ai deux enfants.* — I have two children.

In the negative, the article collapses to *de*, the same rule as the partitive:

> *Je n'ai pas d'enfants.* — I don't have any children.
>
> *Je n'ai pas de frère.* — I don't have a brother.

This is easy to forget when you are speaking, but it is correct, and speakers notice.

## Order in the family

- *l'aîné(e)* — the eldest
- *le cadet / la cadette* — the younger (or youngest) child
- *fils unique / fille unique* — only son / daughter
- *enfant unique* — only child

> *Je suis fille unique.* — I'm an only child (female).
