# Nationalities: Masculine, Feminine, and Lowercase

A nationality is an adjective in French, and adjectives agree. *Il est américain*, but *elle est américaine*. Nationalities also follow two conventions that surprise English speakers: no article after *être* and no capital letter.

## No article

> *Je suis américain.* — I am American.
>
> *Elle est canadienne.* — She is Canadian.

This is the same rule as for professions. You do not say *Je suis un américain* when stating nationality as a description.

## Forming the feminine

Most nationalities add **-e**, and the final consonant becomes audible:

| Masculine | Feminine | Pronunciation change |
|---|---|---|
| *français* | *française* | the *s* becomes /z/ |
| *anglais* | *anglaise* | same |
| *allemand* | *allemande* | the *d* is heard |
| *américain* | *américaine* | the nasal opens |
| *australien* | *australienne* | consonant doubles |
| *canadien* | *canadienne* | same |
| *italien* | *italienne* | same |
| *espagnol* | *espagnole* | the *l* is heard |
| *chinois* | *chinoise* | the *s* becomes /z/ |
| *japonais* | *japonaise* | same |

Endings in **-ien** double the *n* in the feminine (*-ienne*). Adjectives already ending in **-e** (*belge, suisse, russe*) do not change: *un Belge, une Belge*.

## Lowercase for adjectives, capital for people

French treats the adjective and the person differently:

- adjective: *Elle est **f**rançaise.* (lowercase)
- noun for the person: *C'est une **F**rançaise.* (capital)
- language: *Je parle **f**rançais.* (lowercase)

English capitalizes all three. Writing *Je suis Français* is a very common learner error, and it flags immediately.

## Plurals

Add **-s** where the singular does not already end in *s*: *des Canadiens, des Allemandes*. Nationalities that end in *-ais* or *-ois* stay the same in the plural: *les Français, les Chinois*.

## Check yourself

Give the correct form:

1. Marie est ___ . (*allemand*)
2. Mes amis sont ___ . (*italien*, plural)
3. Elle parle ___ . (*japonais*, the language)
4. Je suis ___ . (*français*, you are a woman)

**Answers:** 1. *allemande*. 2. *italiens*. 3. *japonais*. 4. *française*.
