# Masculine and Feminine Professions

Most profession words come in two forms. The patterns are regular enough to learn in one sitting, and a few irregular cases deserve special attention.

## Regular patterns

| Ending | Feminine | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| consonant | add **-e** | *étudiant → étudiante, avocat → avocate* |
| *-ier*, *-er* | *-ière*, *-ère* | *infirmier → infirmière, boulanger → boulangère* |
| *-ien* | *-ienne* | *mécanicien → mécanicienne, musicien → musicienne* |
| *-eur* (from a verb) | *-euse* | *serveur → serveuse, vendeur → vendeuse* |
| *-teur* | *-trice* | *acteur → actrice, directeur → directrice* |
| *-e* already | unchanged | *journaliste, dentiste, architecte, secrétaire* |

The last row is the easy one: *un journaliste, une journaliste*. Only the article changes.

## Jobs with only one form

Several professions have traditionally been masculine words used for everyone:

- *médecin* — *Elle est médecin.* (Often *une médecin* in modern usage.)
- *professeur* — *Elle est professeur.* (Also *professeure*, standard in Québec and gaining ground in France.)
- *ingénieur* — *Elle est ingénieur* or *ingénieure*.
- *écrivain* — *Elle est écrivain* or *écrivaine*.

French usage is changing. In writing, *professeure* and *ingénieure* are increasingly accepted. In speech, the unmarked form is still common. If you are unsure, say what she does instead: *Elle enseigne* ("she teaches") or *Elle est médecin* with the unchanged noun, which is always understood.

## The pronunciation shift

Feminine forms often change the sound, not just the spelling. This is useful for listening:

> *étudiant* (ay-tü-dyahn) → *étudiante* (ay-tü-dyahnt)
>
> *serveur* (ser-VUHR) → *serveuse* (ser-VUHZ)

If you hear a consonant at the end where you expected silence, you are probably hearing a woman's profession.

## How to ask

You can ask directly: *Qu'est-ce que vous faites dans la vie ?* The answer, if she is a woman and you are using a regular noun, will carry the feminine form. You do not need to guess. Learn the pattern, listen for the final consonant, and repeat what you hear.

## Quick drill

Give the feminine form:

1. *un serveur →* ___
2. *un boulanger →* ___
3. *un acteur →* ___
4. *un dentiste →* ___

**Answers:** 1. *une serveuse*. 2. *une boulangère*. 3. *une actrice*. 4. *une dentiste*.
