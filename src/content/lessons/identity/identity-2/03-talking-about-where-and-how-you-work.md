# Where and How You Work: *dans, chez, pour, comme*

Naming a profession is one thing. Explaining where you work, who you work for, and what field you are in is the next step. French has four small words that do most of the work, and each has a specific job.

## Prepositions at work

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *dans* | in (a field) | *Je travaille dans la santé.* |
| *chez* | at (a company or person's place) | *Je travaille chez Renault.* |
| *pour* | for (an employer) | *Je travaille pour une banque.* |
| *comme* | as (a role) | *Je travaille comme serveur.* |

Notice *comme*: **it also drops the article.** *Je travaille comme informaticien*, not *comme un informaticien*.

## Fields you will hear

- *la santé* — healthcare
- *l'éducation* — education
- *la finance* — finance
- *la technologie* or *l'informatique* — IT
- *le commerce* — retail and trade
- *le tourisme* — tourism
- *la restauration* — food service

> *Je travaille dans l'informatique depuis cinq ans.* — I've worked in IT for five years.

*Depuis* with the present tense is how French says "have been doing something for…". You will study it more at B1, but memorize this chunk now.

## Employment status

| French | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Je suis salarié(e)* | I'm an employee |
| *Je suis indépendant(e)* / *à mon compte* | I'm self-employed |
| *Je suis en recherche d'emploi* | I'm job hunting |
| *Je suis au chômage* | I'm unemployed |
| *Je suis à la retraite* | I'm retired |
| *Je suis étudiant(e) en…* | I'm a student of… |

*Étudiant en droit* means law student, *étudiant en médecine* a medical student. The preposition *en* introduces the subject.

## Work hours and places

> *Je travaille à temps plein.* — full time
>
> *Je travaille à mi-temps.* — part time
>
> *Je travaille à la maison.* — from home
>
> *Je travaille en télétravail trois jours par semaine.* — three days of remote work a week

*Le télétravail* is the French word for remote work, and it became very common after 2020.

## Building a mini-profile

Combine the pieces:

> *Je suis infirmière. Je travaille à l'hôpital, dans un service de pédiatrie, à temps plein.*

One sentence for the role, one for the place, one for the schedule. Replace the details with your own, and you have a presentable self-introduction.
