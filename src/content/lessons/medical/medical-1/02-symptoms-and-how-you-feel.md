# Symptoms and How You Feel

Naming the pain is the first step. The second is describing what is wrong in general terms: fever, cough, nausea, tiredness. French uses three different structures for symptoms, and mixing them up is the most common error.

## Structure 1: *avoir* + noun

Most symptoms are things you "have":

| French | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *J'ai de la fièvre.* | I have a fever. |
| *J'ai mal au cœur.* | I feel nauseous. |
| *J'ai la nausée.* | I feel sick. |
| *J'ai des frissons.* | I have chills. |
| *J'ai le vertige.* | I'm dizzy (vertigo). |
| *J'ai la diarrhée.* | I have diarrhea. |
| *J'ai une toux sèche.* | I have a dry cough. |

For temperature, give the number: *J'ai trente-neuf de fièvre* (39 °C).

## Structure 2: *être* + adjective

Some conditions are states:

- *Je suis malade.* — I'm sick.
- *Je suis fatigué(e).* — I'm tired.
- *Je suis enrhumé(e).* — I have a cold.
- *Je suis allergique à…* — I'm allergic to…
- *Je suis enceinte.* — I'm pregnant.

## Structure 3: a verb

A few symptoms are verbs:

> *Je tousse.* — I'm coughing.
>
> *Je vomis.* — I'm vomiting.
>
> *Je saigne.* — I'm bleeding.
>
> *Je tremble.* — I'm shaking.

## *Je me sens…*

This phrase describes how you feel:

> *Je me sens mal.* — I feel ill.
>
> *Je me sens faible.* — I feel weak.
>
> *Je me sens mieux.* — I feel better.

*Je ne me sens pas bien* is a standard, polite way to say you feel unwell.

## How long and how bad

Add duration with *depuis*, and the present tense:

> *J'ai de la fièvre depuis hier.* — I've had a fever since yesterday.
>
> *J'ai mal à la tête depuis deux jours.* — I've had a headache for two days.

And intensity:

- *un peu* — a little
- *très mal* — very painful
- *vraiment mal* — really bad
- *ça fait mal quand je respire* — it hurts when I breathe

## One sentence that does it all

> *Je ne me sens pas bien. J'ai mal au ventre depuis ce matin, et j'ai de la fièvre.*

That sentence combines *se sentir*, *avoir mal*, *depuis*, and *avoir de la fièvre*. If you can say it smoothly, you can describe most common conditions.
