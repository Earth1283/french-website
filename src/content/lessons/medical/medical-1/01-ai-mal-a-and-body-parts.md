# *J'ai mal à…* and the Body

To say something hurts, French does not use a verb for "hurt". It uses a noun phrase: literally, "I have pain at…". Once you have the pattern and the body vocabulary, you can describe almost any ache.

## The pattern

> *J'ai mal à la tête.* — I have a headache.
>
> *J'ai mal au ventre.* — I have a stomachache.
>
> *J'ai mal aux dents.* — I have a toothache.

The *à* that follows *mal* is a normal preposition, so it follows the same contraction rules you learned in the pronunciation lessons:

| Noun | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| masculine singular | *au* | *au dos, au ventre, au bras* |
| feminine singular | *à la* | *à la tête, à la gorge, à la jambe* |
| vowel start | *à l'* | *à l'estomac, à l'oreille, à l'œil* |
| plural | *aux* | *aux dents, aux pieds, aux yeux* |

## Body vocabulary

| Masculine | Feminine | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| *le dos* (back) | *la tête* (head) | *les yeux* (eyes) |
| *le ventre* (belly) | *la gorge* (throat) | *les dents* (teeth) |
| *le bras* (arm) | *la jambe* (leg) | *les pieds* (feet) |
| *le cou* (neck) | *la main* (hand) | *les mains* (hands) |
| *le genou* (knee) | *l'oreille* (ear) | *les oreilles* (ears) |
| *l'estomac* (stomach) | *l'épaule* (shoulder) | *les jambes* (legs) |

*Un œil* becomes *des yeux* in the plural, an irregular form you should memorize: *J'ai mal aux yeux.*

## Specific places

To point out a body part, use *ici* and *là*:

> *Ça fait mal ici.* — It hurts here.
>
> *J'ai mal là.* — I have pain there.

Pointing with a gesture and saying *ici* is a complete and efficient sentence in any doctor's office.

## Two traps

**1. *Le cœur*.** *J'ai mal au cœur* does not mean heartache. It means **I feel nauseous.** If you have chest pain, say *J'ai mal à la poitrine* (chest) or *J'ai une douleur dans la poitrine*.

**2. *Mal de…*.** You will also hear *J'ai un mal de tête* or *un mal de gorge* in a more literary or descriptive way. For everyday speech, *j'ai mal à…* is the form to learn first.

## Short drill

Complete each sentence:

1. J'ai mal ___ dos.
2. Elle a mal ___ gorge.
3. Il a mal ___ pieds.
4. Nous avons mal ___ oreille.

**Answers:** 1. *au*. 2. *à la*. 3. *aux*. 4. *à l'*.
