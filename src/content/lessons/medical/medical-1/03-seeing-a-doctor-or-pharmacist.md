# Seeing a Doctor or a Pharmacist

Knowing where to go and what you will be asked makes the visit much less stressful. This chapter covers who to see for what, and the questions you will hear.

## Where to go

| Situation | Go to |
|---|---|
| minor ailment, cold, mild pain | *la pharmacie* |
| need advice or a diagnosis | *le médecin généraliste* (GP) |
| urgent but not life-threatening | *les urgences* (emergency room) |
| emergency | call *le 15* (SAMU) or *le 112* |

In France, the **pharmacist** is trained to advise on minor problems and is a good first step. Look for the green cross.

## Making an appointment

> *Je voudrais prendre rendez-vous avec le docteur Martin.*
>
> I would like to make an appointment with Dr. Martin.
>
> *Est-ce qu'il y a une consultation aujourd'hui ?*
>
> Is there a consultation available today?

Many doctors use online booking, but a phone call or a walk-in at certain clinics is also common.

## What the doctor asks

You will hear the *vous* form, usually in questions:

| Question | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Qu'est-ce qui ne va pas ?* | What's wrong? |
| *Où avez-vous mal ?* | Where does it hurt? |
| *Depuis quand ?* | Since when? |
| *Avez-vous de la fièvre ?* | Do you have a fever? |
| *Avez-vous des allergies ?* | Do you have any allergies? |
| *Vous prenez des médicaments ?* | Are you taking any medication? |

## What the doctor says

Doctors use the imperative:

> *Respirez profondément.* — Breathe deeply.
>
> *Toussez.* — Cough.
>
> *Montrez-moi où ça fait mal.* — Show me where it hurts.
>
> *Ouvrez la bouche.* — Open your mouth.

## At the pharmacy

> *Bonjour, j'ai mal à la gorge. Qu'est-ce que vous me conseillez ?*
>
> Hello, I have a sore throat. What do you recommend?

*Qu'est-ce que vous me conseillez ?* is the key question. The pharmacist will recommend something, and may say *Il vous faut une ordonnance* if the product needs a prescription.

## Practical note

If you are a visitor, ask early about payment and insurance. In France, patients often pay at the consultation and are reimbursed later, so keep the receipt (*la feuille de soins*). For travelers, check your insurance before departure.
