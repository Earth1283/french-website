# Y: One Letter Erases a Whole Place

*Y* is the smallest word in French grammar and one of the most useful. It replaces *à + a place* or *à + a thing* — never a person. Once you can hear "à + [something non-human]" coming, *y* becomes automatic.

> *Tu vas à la pharmacie ?* — *Oui, j'y vais.*
>
> *Tu penses à ton examen ?* — *Oui, j'y pense.*
>
> *Tu joues au tennis ?* — *Oui, j'y joue.*

## Places: the most common use

Any destination or location introduced by *à*, *au*, *à la*, *à l'*, *aux*, *dans*, *chez*, *en* (for countries) — anywhere, really — can become *y*:

> *Tu habites en France ?* — *Oui, j'y habite.*
>
> *Il est chez le dentiste ?* — *Oui, il y est.*

This is the sense English speakers pick up fastest, because English has a rough equivalent: "there." *J'y vais* — "I'm going (there)."

## Things: the sense that trips people up

*Y* also replaces *à + a thing*, after verbs like *penser à*, *réfléchir à*, *s'intéresser à*, *jouer à*. Here English has no equivalent word at all — "I think about it" just leaves "it" hanging at the end, but French pulls the whole prepositional phrase to the front as *y*:

> *Tu réfléchis à sa proposition ?* — *Oui, j'y réfléchis.*
>
> *Tu t'intéresses à la politique ?* — *Oui, je m'y intéresse.*

## The one hard rule: never for people

*Y* cannot replace *à + a person*. For a person, you either use *lui/leur* (with verbs like *penser à quelqu'un*'s cousins *parler à*, *téléphoner à*) or, for a small set of verbs including *penser à*, a stressed pronoun after *à*:

> *Tu penses à ton frère ?* — *Oui, je pense à lui.* (never *j'y pense*, about a person)
>
> *Tu parles à ton frère ?* — *Oui, je lui parle.*

*Penser à* is the trickiest verb here because it behaves one way for things (*y*) and another way for people (*à + stressed pronoun*) — most other *à*-verbs just take *lui/leur* regardless.

## Fixed chunks worth memorizing whole

Some expressions with *y* are frozen and not built compositionally — learn them as single units:

- *il y a* — there is / there are
- *vas-y / allez-y* — go on / go ahead
- *ça y est* — that's it / it's done

## Quick check

- *Tu vas au marché ?* — *Oui, j'___ vais.* (y)
- *Tu penses à ta grand-mère ?* — *Oui, je pense à ___.* (elle)
- *Il y a combien de personnes ?* — what does *il y a* mean here? (there are / how many people are there)
