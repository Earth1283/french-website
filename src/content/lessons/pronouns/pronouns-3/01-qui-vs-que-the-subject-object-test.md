# Qui vs Que: The Subject/Object Test

A relative clause welds two sentences into one by replacing a repeated noun with a connecting word. French has one word for when that noun is the *subject* of the new clause, and a different word for when it's the *object* — and mixing them up is the single most common relative-pronoun error at this level.

> *J'ai un ami. Cet ami parle japonais.* → *J'ai un ami **qui** parle japonais.*
>
> *Je lis un livre. J'aime beaucoup ce livre.* → *Je lis un livre **que** j'aime beaucoup.*

## The test

Take the noun you're about to replace and ask: in the second sentence, is it *doing* the verb, or *receiving* the verb?

- Doing the verb → **qui** (subject)
- Receiving the verb → **que** (object)

*Cet ami* is doing the speaking → *qui*. *Ce livre* is not doing any liking — the speaker is — so the book is the object → *que*.

A mechanical shortcut: look at what comes right after the relative pronoun. If a verb comes immediately after, you need *qui* (the pronoun itself is the subject). If a subject noun or pronoun comes right after instead, you need *que* (the clause already has its subject, so the pronoun must be the object).

> *le livre **qui** est sur la table* (verb right after → qui)
>
> *le livre **que** je lis* (subject "je" right after → que)

## Elision: only one of them does it

*Que* elides to *qu'* before a vowel sound: *l'ami qu'il connaît*. *Qui* never elides, even before a vowel: *l'ami qui arrive*, not *"l'ami qu'arrive."* This small spelling detail is an easy way to catch your own mistakes when proofreading.

## Both can refer to people or things

Unlike English, which sometimes distinguishes "who" (people) from "which" (things), French *qui* and *que* cover both — the subject/object test is the only thing that matters, not what kind of noun you're replacing:

> *La voiture qui est garée là* — the car that's parked there (thing, subject)
>
> *La personne que tu cherches* — the person you're looking for (person, object)

## Quick check

- *Le film ___ passe ce soir m'intéresse.* (qui — subject of "passe")
- *Le film ___ tu as recommandé était nul.* (que — object; "tu" is the subject)
- *C'est la femme ___ travaille avec moi.* (qui)
